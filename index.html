How to Play & Official Rules

  • ⚡ 1s UNO Penalty: Hit "SAY UNO!" within 1 second of having 1 card left, or draw 2 penalty cards.
  • 🏳️ Resigning: Resigning forfeits XP. In 2-player matches, resigning instantly grants the remaining player the win!
  • 🎯 Objective: Be the first player to empty your hand of all cards.
  • 🃏 Exhaustible Deck: Played cards leave the active pool permanently until the deck runs out and reshuffles.
  • ⊘ Skip Card: The next player in rotation loses their turn.
  • ⇄ Reverse Card: Reverses play direction. In 2-player matches, it acts as a Skip.
  • ＋2 Draw Two: Forces the next player to draw 2 cards and lose their turn.
  • ★ Wild & +4: Play anytime to choose active color (and force next player to draw 4 on Wild Draw 4).
  • 🤖 Solo Mode: Play against RoboRobbins 🤖 starting fresh at Level 1 with full stat saving!
