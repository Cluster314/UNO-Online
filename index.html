How to Play & Rules

  • ⚡ 1s UNO Penalty: Hit "SAY UNO!" within 1 second of having 1 card left, or draw 2 penalty cards.
  • 🔄 2-Player Reverse: Playing a Reverse card in a 2-player match acts as a Skip, giving you another turn.
  • 🏆 XP Progression: Win matches and accumulate points from leftover opponent hands to rank up your profile level.
  • 🤖 Solo Mode: Choose "Play vs Computer" for an instant solo match against RoboRobbins 🤖 starting fresh at Level 1.
